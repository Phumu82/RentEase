# RentEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

**What we collect.** Account details (name, email, phone, role), university and student or business details you enter, documents you upload for verification, booking, payment-status, message and maintenance records, a device token for push notifications, and your language and notification settings.

**Why.** To create your account, show and manage listings and bookings, verify landlords, send notifications, and keep the service safe.

**Where it is stored.** In our hosted Supabase database and storage. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Push messages are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google.

**Sharing.** Landlords see a student's booking details and documents only for bookings that student makes with them. We do not sell personal information.

**Your choices.** You can edit your profile, change notification settings, and ask us to delete your account and data by emailing YOUR EMAIL.
